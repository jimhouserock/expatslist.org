# ExpatsList

ExpatsList (expatslist.org) is a directory and community platform for expats, newcomers, and remote workers living abroad. It helps people relocating to or already living in a country find local, expat-friendly businesses and services, and connect with each other through community questions, recommendations, and classifieds. Coverage spans Cambodia, Canada, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Indonesia, Laos, Mexico, Panama, Poland, Puerto Rico, Spain, Thailand, the USA, and Vietnam, with dedicated pages for major expat destination cities in each country.

## Finding a business or service

Visitors browse by country (for example, the Mexico page) or by city, then narrow down by category: immigration lawyers, real estate agents, doctors, dentists, schools, movers, accountants, coworking spaces, and more. Search finds listings directly by name, service, or location, so a user doesn't need to know which city page a business sits on. Each listing shows the business's services, location, and a way to get in touch.

## Listing or claiming a business

A business owner adds their business through the "List Your Business" flow. If a business is already listed but not yet managed by its owner, the owner can claim it to take control of the listing, update its details, and respond as the business.

## Verification

Businesses can apply for a verified badge, which signals to visitors that the listing has been confirmed as real and active. It's a quick trust signal when comparing several listings in the same category.

## Community questions

The Questions section lets people ask about visa rules, neighborhoods, cost of living, schools, and healthcare, and get answers from residents, other newcomers, and locals. Think of it as a Q&A board scoped to expat life in each city or country.

## Recommendations

The Recommendations section surfaces businesses and services other users vouch for, useful when someone wants a trusted starting point instead of browsing a category cold.

## Classifieds

Classifieds cover buy/sell, housing, jobs, and community postings. Legitimate ones always include a direct way to make contact: a phone number, email address, WhatsApp number, or external link. A listing with no direct contact method isn't something a user can act on.

## Safety

The Safety section has guidance on using the platform safely, including how to vet a listing or classified before meeting someone or sending money.

## Accounts

Users can create an account to manage their profile and any listings or classifieds they've posted.
